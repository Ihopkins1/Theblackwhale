About Us

The Black Whale was built as a premium seafood platform for people seeking luxury fish, whether they are sourcing for their business or preparing a memorable dinner with family.

Why We Built It

We wanted one place where quality fish is the standard, not the exception. Every part of the experience is designed so customers can discover trusted products, place orders with confidence, and return knowing they will receive high-quality seafood every time.

Team Contributions

CSS and visual design was led mostly by Adonis Pearson and Kayron, shaping the look and overall motion of the storefront.

Core functionality and feature workflows were developed by Ishaan Hopkins and Byron Ross, including account flows, order logic, and dashboard behavior.

Our Quality Promise

  • Maintain a curated marketplace focused on premium fish selections.
  • Provide clear order and fulfillment visibility from checkout to delivery.
  • Keep the platform simple enough for family dining and strong enough for business sourcing.
